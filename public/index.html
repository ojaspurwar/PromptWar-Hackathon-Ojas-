HOW IT WORKS

Focus on the important stuff.

Inbox Sprint helps you review email in small, focused batches.

  1. AI reads the room. Messages get a priority estimate and a quick summary.
  2. You make the call. Keep, archive, or trash — nothing happens without your confirmation.
  3. Build momentum. Clear the low-signal stuff and give important mail the attention it needs.
Sample inbox is safe to explore. Connect Gmail when you’re ready to take real actions.
